About Kokab Fodool

Kokab Fodool is a learning project for children ages 3 to 10, combining short animated stories with play and discovery.

Meet the Kokab Fodool characters

Lumaa teaches letters and numbers, Fudool sets riddles, ROBO explores experiments, and Mishaal discovers geography and nature. The What If? character begins each adventure with a question about our world.

Contact for adults

Parents and teachers can reach the project through the official Kokab Fodool Instagram account.

Instagram → TikTok

How families use the platform

Start with one episode, then choose a game on the same skill by age. Printable worksheets carry the activity into the home or classroom.

The series, games and app

Kokab Fodool publishes episodes on its official channel. The site brings the learning materials and the offline-capable Android app together.
