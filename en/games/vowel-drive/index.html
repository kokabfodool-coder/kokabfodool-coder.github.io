How to play and what you practise

Listen to the syllable, steer to the gate with the matching vowel and press Drive. Later rounds ask you to fill the missing vowel in a word.

  • 3–5 yearsListen and choose the vowel gate (Levels 1–3)
  • 6–7 yearsDistinguish vowels, fill in the missing vowel and read sukūn (Levels 4–15)
