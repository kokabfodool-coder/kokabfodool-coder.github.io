How to play and what you practise

Put the requested products in the basket and read their prices. Choose coins to pay or give change. Other rounds compare offers or check a receipt.

  • 6–7 yearsAdd prices and pay with coins (Levels 1–15)
  • 8–10 yearsCompare deals, check receipts and calculate change (Levels 16–18)
