How to play and what you practise

Tap each object once as you count, then choose the total. Use groups to reason through addition and subtraction before moving to multiplication and division.

  • 3–5 yearsCount groups up to 10 (Levels 1–2)
  • 6–7 yearsAdd, subtract and order numbers (Levels 3–18)
  • 8–10 yearsMultiply, divide and solve two-step problems (Levels 19–21)
