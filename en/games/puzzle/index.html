How to play and what you practise

Study the reference picture and move the pieces into place. On later levels, rotate pieces to match the picture’s orientation.

  • 3–5 yearsRebuild pictures with 4 or 6 pieces (Levels 1–4)
  • 6–7 yearsRebuild 9-piece pictures (Levels 5–7)
  • 8–10 yearsRebuild pictures and rotate pieces to fit (Levels 8–12)
