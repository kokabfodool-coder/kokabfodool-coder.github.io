Frequently asked questions

Answers about age paths, offline play, watching episodes and saving progress on Kokab Fodool.

How do I pick a starting level?

Open a game and choose one of its age paths. Play starts at the first level in that path, and a learner can continue from progress saved on that device.

Do the games work offline?

The Android app includes the games and the files they need. In the browser, download the offline game pack before going offline. Episodes and external links require an internet connection.

How do I print a worksheet or get help?

Select a worksheet in the parents and teachers section and open its printer-friendly page or PDF. An adult can contact the official account for help.
