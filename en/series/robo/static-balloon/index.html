Why does paper stick to a balloon?

Watch little pieces of paper jump toward ROBO’s balloon.

Watch on YouTube

YouTube loads when you choose to play the video.

Learning goal

Describe what happened and use an observation to explain the result.

Simple science experiments for childrenRobo Lab

Why does it happen?

Rubbing a balloon with cloth gives it a static charge. That charge can attract little pieces of paper.

Try it yourself

Ask an adult to rub a balloon with cloth. Compare what the paper does before and after rubbing.

Try this with an adult. Keep uninflated and broken balloons away from young children.
