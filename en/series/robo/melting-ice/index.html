Watch ice with ROBO and discover how water changes from a solid into a liquid.

Simple science experiments for childrenRobo Lab

Why does it happen?

When heat flows into ice, the ice can melt into liquid water.

Try it yourself

Put an ice cube on each of two plates. Keep one in a warm room and one in a cooler spot. Compare them after five minutes.

Try with an adult and wipe up any spilled water.
