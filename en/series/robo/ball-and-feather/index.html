Which falls first, the ball or the feather?

Predict what happens in ROBO’s race between a ball and a feather.

Learning goal

Describe what happened and use an observation to explain the result.

Simple science experiments for childrenRobo Lab

Why does it happen?

Gravity acts on both objects. In this experiment, air resistance slows the feather more relative to its weight.

Try it yourself

From a low height, drop a flat sheet of paper and a matching crumpled sheet together. Compare how they fall.
