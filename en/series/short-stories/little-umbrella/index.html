The Little Umbrella

A rabbit stands in the rain. A little fox invites it to share an umbrella.

Watch on YouTube

YouTube loads when you choose to play the video.

Learning goal

Put two events in order and describe the character’s choice.

Fudool PuzzleFor parents and teachers

What did we learn?

The fox noticed the rabbit needed help and shared its umbrella. That small action showed kindness.

Try it yourself

Draw the umbrella and who is standing under it. Tell someone about one thing you can share with a friend.
