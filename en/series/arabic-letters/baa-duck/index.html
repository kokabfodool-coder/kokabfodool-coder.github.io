Arabic letter Baa with a duck

Learn the Arabic letter baa with Lumaa. Look for its single dot and hear the ba sound in the Arabic word for duck.

Learning goal

Recognise the letter sound and find it in the picture word.

بَبَطَّة

What did we learn?

Baa has one dot below it. The short a mark above it gives us ba, the first sound in بَطَّة (battah, duck).

Try it yourself

Write بَ on paper. Cover the dot with your finger, then uncover it and say battah. Try baab (door) too.
