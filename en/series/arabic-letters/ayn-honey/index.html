Arabic letter Ayn with honey

Explore the Arabic letter ayn with Lumaa. Hear the first sound in the Arabic word for honey and try a picture activity.

Learning goal

Recognise the letter sound and find it in the picture word.

عَعَسَل

What did we learn?

The short a mark over ayn gives عَ. Both عَسَل (asal, honey) and عَلَم (alam, flag) start with it.
