Arabic letter Zay with a giraffe

Listen to زَ with Lumaa and practice the Arabic word for a giraffe.

Learning goal

Recognise the letter sound and find it in the picture word.

زَزَرَافَة

What did we learn?

Zay has one dot above it. Compare رَ and زَ and say zarāfah, the Arabic word for giraffe.
