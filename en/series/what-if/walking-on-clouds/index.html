What if you could walk on clouds?

Imagine a walk above the clouds, then learn what clouds are made of.

Watch on YouTube

YouTube loads when you choose to play the video.

Learning goal

Explain how a change in the situation could affect the result.

Simple science experiments for childrenJourney to Space

In real life

A cloud is made of tiny water droplets or ice crystals in the air. They cannot support our weight.

Try it yourself

Watch the clouds from a window and sketch a shape you see. Imagine how it might change over time.
