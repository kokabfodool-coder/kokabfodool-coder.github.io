My First Journey with Arabic Letters

A free printable kit for ages 4 to 6. Learn 12 Arabic letters, from Alif to Seen, in 4 weeks.

What is in the kit?

23 A4 pages with a weekly plan, a parent guide, tracing and coloring worksheets, matching cards, links to episodes and games, and a final review.
