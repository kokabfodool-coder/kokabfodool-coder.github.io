Privacy

How games, video links and contact work on Kokab Fodool.

Learning and games

Games and activities run in the browser. Stars, levels, and the last completed round are saved in this browser. You can clear game progress from your browser’s site data settings. Activities are free to use without entering personal details or opening a site account.

Local explorer profiles

Optional nicknames, character choices and age groups are saved on this device only. An installed device voice reads the nickname in greetings and congratulations. Exported progress files contain completed levels and stars. No account, cloud sync or transmission of these fields is used. Clear the site data to remove them. The Android preview has separate local storage from your browser.

External services

Episode and playlist buttons open YouTube. The adult contact link opens Instagram.

Hosting and measurement

The hosting service processes standard connection information to deliver and protect the pages. This version operates without advertising tools or behavioral analytics.

Contact

The official contact link is for parents and teachers. An adult should handle any communication and avoid sending children’s personal information.

Google and YouTube privacy · Instagram privacy

Children’s content and links

The games do not require a site account. Game progress and settings are saved locally. Episode covers load from this site, and the YouTube player connects to YouTube when a visitor requests playback. External sites apply their own policies when opened.

Manage local data

An adult can remove progress and downloaded game files through the site or app data settings on the device. Use the adult contact link for a privacy question.

Parent account and progress backups

A parent or guardian can create an optional account in the Android app. Google Firebase uses the adult’s email address for sign-in and account verification. When the adult chooses to save a backup, the service stores completed-level scores and stars. The child’s name and age and the audio settings stay on the device. Backups remain available until the adult deletes the account and backups from the app’s settings.
