Teaching Arabic short vowels

Teach fatḥa, kasra and ḍamma through listening, picture words and steering to the correct gate in the vowel game.

Compare one vowel at a time

Show بَ, بِ and بُ. Say each sound slowly and point to its mark. Ask the child to find بَ after hearing it, and link it to بَطَّة (duck). Use بِ in بِنت and بُ in بُرتقال for the other sounds.

Fill a gap in a familiar word

Show بَطَّة and then write the word again with the first vowel mark removed. Ask the child to name the mark they heard. Sound matching works for younger learners; filling the gap suits early readers.

Hear sukūn within a word

Say شَمْس (sun). The mīm is marked with sukūn and has no short vowel of its own. Listen to the whole word before looking at the marks.
