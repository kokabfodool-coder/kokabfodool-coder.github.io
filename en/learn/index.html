Children’s learning guide

Combine episodes, age-based games and worksheets to explore letters, numbers and science with children aged 3 to 10.

Choose a task by age and confidence

At ages 3–5, start with a picture, a sound and one question. At ages 6–7, ask the child to explain a choice or write an example. At ages 8–10, use a multi-step problem or ask for a prediction before an experiment.

Move from watching to doing

Watch one short episode and pause at its main idea. Ask what the child noticed, choose a game on the same skill and finish with a worksheet or a simple activity at home.
