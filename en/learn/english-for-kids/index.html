English letters and numbers activities for children

Connect familiar English words with letter sounds and practise saying and matching numbers in short games.

Connect the letter, sound and picture

Put an apple picture next to A and a ball next to B. Say apple and ball, then ask the child to match each first sound to its picture. Add more examples once the first two are familiar.

Pair uppercase and lowercase

Write A and a on separate cards, then B and b. Ask the child to pair both forms of each letter and find them in a familiar word.

Hear a number and build it

Say one, two or three and ask the child to place that many blocks. Count the blocks together and pair the spoken number with its numeral.
