Teaching Arabic letters to children

Start with an Arabic letter sound and a picture word, then practise letter position and handwriting with a game and a worksheet.

Listen for the first sound

Show pictures of a lion and a duck. Say أَسَد (asad) and بَطَّة (baṭṭa). Ask which picture begins with أَ, then let the child say the word. Check the sound using a second word before moving on.

Look for the letter in a word

With early readers, look for the letter ب in بَطَّة, حَبْل and كِتاب. Ask where it appears before tracing or copying one of the words.

Review the writing

Use a letter worksheet to move from a model to the dotted line and then an empty line. Ask the learner which part felt easy and which part they want to try again.
