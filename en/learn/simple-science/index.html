Simple science experiments for children

Test how water temperature affects melting ice and compare results with Robo Lab and the science series.

Predict and test

Use two matching cups and two similar ice cubes. Put equal amounts of fridge-cold water and room-temperature water in the cups. Ask which cube the child expects to melt first.

Change one thing at a time

Start a timer after adding the ice. Compare the cube sizes after a minute, and record what you see. Use the same cups and amounts when you repeat the test so water temperature is the factor being compared.

Explain the result from the evidence

Ask the child to finish the sentence “I observed that…”. Older children can record the times in a table and compare trials. You can try the same steps first in the in-game simulation.
