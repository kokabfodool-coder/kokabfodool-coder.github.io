Educational content policy

How Kokab Fodool organises episodes, games and worksheets by skill and age, and how to report a content issue.

Match the task to the learner

Game pages show skills and age paths according to the material. Families can choose the path that fits the learner. Early paths focus on letter and vowel recognition, while suitable older paths use multi-step problems and experiments.

Examples and instructions are reviewable

Letters appear alongside picture words, numbers stay legible, and simulations label illustrative results. Share the round name if you notice unclear pronunciation or a question with more than one valid answer.
